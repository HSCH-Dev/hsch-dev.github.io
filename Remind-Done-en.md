# Privacy Policy – Remind Until Done

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Remind Until Done (the "App") is an offline-only reminder app for Windows. This policy explains what information the App handles.

## 1\. Information we collect

The App does not collect or transmit any information to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics, or crash-report uploads.

## 2\. Information stored on your PC

The following information is stored only on your PC:



Reminder text, text color, font size, order, completion history, language, notification status, notification interval, notification sound on/off, background color, startup option, window position and size, whether the countdown is shown, and whether history times are shown.



This data is kept in storage specific to your Windows user account and is not shared with other users or any outside party.

## 3\. Transmission of data

The App does not connect to the internet and does not send data anywhere. It also does not write reminder text or personal information to logs.

## 4\. Permissions

The App does not require administrator rights and does not access your camera, microphone, location, or contacts. Starting with Windows can be turned on or off at any time in the Settings screen.

## 5\. Deleting your data

* You can delete reminders in the App. You can select and delete completion history on the History screen.
* If you installed the App from the Microsoft Store, uninstalling it also removes the stored data.

## 6\. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised.

## 7\. Contact

For inquiries, please use the [Inquiry form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdUvIss_TMLNz5sdeBGiFMfMXdJAOuCLQU2sIP7hVNGzNE4pQ/viewform?usp=header). We collect the content of your inquiry (including any information you choose to provide) only if you contact us. If you enter a reply email address, we also collect that information.



The collected information is used only to respond to and communicate with you about your inquiry, and to improve the App and investigate defects. Inquiry information is sent and stored through Google Forms, and the developer does not provide or disclose it to any third party.



Inquiry information is kept for as long as needed to handle your inquiry or investigate a defect, and is deleted once it is no longer needed. If you would like your information deleted, please contact us through the inquiry form. If we cannot identify the relevant information, we cannot delete it, and if no reply email address was provided, we cannot notify you of the result.
