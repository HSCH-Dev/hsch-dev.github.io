# Date Hider for YouTube Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

"Date Hider for YouTube" (the "Extension") is a Google Chrome extension for desktop. On YouTube, it hides only the upload date of videos published before a period you choose. This policy explains what information the Extension handles.

## 1. Information we use

To decide whether to hide an upload date, the Extension reads the upload-date text shown on YouTube ([www.youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com)) pages. This text is used only on your device. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

The Extension also saves the following settings, which you choose, in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

- The period to hide, as a number and a unit (weeks, months or years)
- The ON/OFF switch

No other information is saved. These settings are not synced with your Google account or other devices.

## 2. Information we do not collect

The Extension does not collect:

- Personal information such as your name or email address
- Browsing, search or watch history
- Your YouTube or Google account information
- Page content such as video titles, channel names or comments

The Extension does not connect to any external server.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share or disclose your data to any third party. Nothing is used for advertising, tracking or analytics.

## 4. Storage and deletion

Settings are stored only in your browser. They are overwritten when you change them in the settings popup, and deleted when you uninstall the Extension.

## 5. Permissions

The Extension uses only the minimum permissions it needs:

- storage: to save the settings described above.
- Access to https://www.youtube.com/*: to change how upload dates are displayed on YouTube pages.

## 6. Unofficial extension

The Extension is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date on this page.
