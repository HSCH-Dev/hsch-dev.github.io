# Privacy Policy - Date Hider for YouTube

Last updated: October 26, 2026

"Date Hider for YouTube" (the "Extension") is a Google Chrome extension for desktop. On YouTube, it hides only the upload date of videos published before a period you choose. This policy explains what information the Extension handles.

## 1\. Information we use

To decide whether to hide an upload date, the Extension reads the upload-date text shown on YouTube ([www.youtube.com](https://www.youtube.com)) pages. This text is used only on your device. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

The Extension also saves the following settings, which you choose, in the browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local):

* The period to hide, as a number and a unit (weeks, months or years)
* The ON/OFF switch

No other information is saved. These settings are not synced with your Google account or other devices.

## 2\. Information we do not collect

In connection with the use of the Extension, we do not collect the following information:

* Personal information such as your name or email address
* Browsing, search or watch history
* Your YouTube or Google account information
* Page content such as video titles, channel names or comments
* Cookies and information stored in cookies

The Extension itself does not connect to any external server.

## 3\. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide or disclose your data to any third party. Nothing is used for advertising, tracking or analytics.

## 4\. Storage and deletion

Settings are stored only in your browser. They are overwritten when you change them in the settings popup, and deleted when you uninstall the Extension.

## 5\. Permissions

The Extension uses only the minimum permissions it needs:

* storage: to save the settings described above.
* Access to [https://www.youtube.com/\*](https://www.youtube.com/): to change how upload dates are displayed on YouTube pages.

## 6\. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when needed. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date on this page.

## 7\. Information obtained when you contact us

Please contact us through the [inquiry form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdUvIss_TMLNz5sdeBGiFMfMXdJAOuCLQU2sIP7hVNGzNE4pQ/viewform?usp=header). Only when you contact us, we obtain the content of your inquiry (including any information you choose to write in it). If you enter a reply-to email address, we also obtain that address.

We use the information we obtain only to reply to and contact you about your inquiry, and to improve the Extension and investigate bugs. Inquiry information is sent and stored through Google Forms, and the developer does not provide or disclose it to any third party.

Inquiry information is kept for as long as needed to respond to the inquiry or investigate a bug, and is deleted when it is no longer needed. If you would like it deleted, please contact us through the inquiry form. We cannot delete the information if we cannot identify it, and we cannot notify you of the result if you did not provide a reply-to email address.
